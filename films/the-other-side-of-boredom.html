Slow and Spurious Films

The Other Side of Boredom

2024  ·  13 mins

There is a world of rapture on the other side of boredom.

A single-shot film documenting an unremarkable morning jog becomes a history of our species and a touching personal skirmish with the truth of ageing. A single-shot, zero-budget experimental documentary by a 53-year-old first-time film maker. Go figure.
