About

Craig Taylor

“I publish as C. M. Taylor because another writer got to ‘Craig Taylor’ first — and, to make it worse, he’s a really nice guy.”

I grew up in England, have lived in India, Spain and Belgium, and have long since settled in Oxfordshire with my wife and two daughters.

I began as a novelist and still call myself one — but these days I’m just as taken with screenwriting. I’ve co-written a feature film, been commissioned to adapt two of my own novels for the screen, and I teach the next generation of storytellers at the Oxford International Centre for Publishing.

Along the way I’ve ghostwritten for an internationally best-selling author, written for The Guardian and The Daily Telegraph, and smuggled a few words onto Plan B’s album The Ballad of Belmarsh.

I write in a shed at the bottom of the garden in Eynsham, six miles north-west of Oxford. I call it my battery — it’s where I go to charge back up.
